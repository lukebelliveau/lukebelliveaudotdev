me, in a nutshell

Check out my recent platform engineering work.

Welcome to my corner of the internet! My name is Luke. I'm a platform engineer from the USA, currently splitting time between Barcelona and the Boston area.

After getting my Computer Science degree from UCONN in 2016, I spent a couple of years in Chicago working at ThoughtWorks in domains like insurance administration, e-commerce, and cybersecurity. On my teams I tended to steward our test automation and CI/CD pipelines, getting early exposure to infrastructure and developer experience work.

I went on to work on the Cloud Infrastructure team at GrubHub in NYC. Migrating to a custom-built infrastructure platform reminiscent of the systems at Netflix, they needed somebody with system design & architecture chops. I worked on the Java frameworks & services consumed by application developers to build, test & deploy their applications. I also built GUIs for Site Reliability Engineers to expose network controls and insights during outages. My last major project was the architecture proposal for a new notifications platform - Java backend, TypeScript frontend, Cassandra database - which was approved by the org-wide architecture review council.

In January 2020, I took a sabbatical and went backpacking around Europe, blissfully unaware of how the year would unfold. I spent a few months volunteering at a hostel in Hungary until the pandemic happened. Hunkered down in Budapest, I took on shorter term contracts - including rebuilding the website for Selenium, the popular open source browser automation tool.

In 2021, I dove back into software as an independent consultant. I worked with Nuwave Technologies on their API gateway product and built ZUUT, a CAD tool for cannabis cultivation planning.

Since 2023, I've focused on platform engineering. At Wendy's, I served as liaison between web and platform teams during their AWS to GCP migration, implemented GitOps deployments with ArgoCD, and contributed to FreshAI, their AI-driven drive-thru ordering system now deployed at 160+ restaurants.

I then led the architecture of a data platform for an autonomous robotics company. Their welding robots generate massive amounts of data from laser scanners and computer vision systems - I built the platform that lets their AI teams focus on robotics instead of debugging data pipelines. Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Terraform, delivered over ~8 months.

After that, I built the cloud-native platform on GCP behind a large enterprise's gamification product - Terraform, Kubernetes, GitOps with ArgoCD, dual-region delivery, and end-to-end observability. The product launched in June 2026, with the platform serving 5.8 million requests at a 99.986% success rate in its first week.

Today I'm building an agentic infrastructure platform. Applications declare what they need, the platform generates and runs their infrastructure and delivery, and an engineering agent investigates alerts and prepares tested fixes for review.

I am currently open to contract, part time and full time work. If you think we may be a good fit, I'd love to hear from you!
